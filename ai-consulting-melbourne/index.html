What does an AI consultant in Melbourne do?

We start by asking the people doing the work how the business really runs: which tasks repeat, how often, and how long each one takes.

We put hours and dollars on what we find, so you can see which fixes are worth doing.

Then we help with the ones you choose, whether that is a digital support assistant, search work or something simpler.

Who we work with

Businesses with roughly 15 to 150 staff and a lot of repeatable admin.

Owner-led, or run by an owner and a general manager. The week fills up with quotes, bookings, scheduling, invoicing and enquiries. We start with these seven kinds of business, and give each the same weight.

Trades and construction

Quotes, job scheduling, variations and invoicing.

Multi-practitioner clinics

Bookings, reminders, intake forms and rescheduling.

Real estate

Enquiries, inspection bookings, follow-ups and listing paperwork.

NDIS providers

Scheduling, shift notes, plan paperwork and invoicing.

Logistics and transport

Quotes, bookings, dispatch scheduling and invoicing.

Manufacturers and fabricators

Quotes, job paperwork, ordering and invoicing.

Auto repair

Bookings, quotes, parts ordering and invoicing.
