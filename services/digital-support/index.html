Digital support

An assistant that answers when your team cannot.

Many of the questions your business gets are the same ones, again and again. Opening hours, pricing, how to book, where an order is, how a process works. A digital support assistant answers them by chat on your site or by phone, so your team can spend its time on the harder things. It can face customers, face your own staff, or do both.

What it can do

Answer common questions

From your own material, in your own wording.

Take messages and requests

And pass them to the right person with the details filled in.

Book and reschedule

Where your booking system allows it. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm which booking systems MarvelUs integrates with]

Work after hours

So enquiries are not left until morning.

Support your own staff

With answers to "how do we do this here" questions.

Hand over to a person

When a customer is upset, when it is unsure, or when the matter is too important to guess.

Chat or voice

Chat

Sits on your website or inside the tools you already use. Good for written questions and for people who want a record.

Voice

Answers the phone, takes the details and routes the call. Good for trades, clinics and anywhere the phone rings while hands are busy.

Voice assistants always say they are AI at the start of a call. [[PH:legal|confirm disclosure wording and Australian call recording and consent requirements with a legal adviser]]

How it is set up

  1. 01

    Map the questions.

    We list what people really ask and what the right answers are.

  2. 02

    Write the knowledge.

    We build the answers from your material and have you check every one.

  3. 03

    Test hard.

    We try awkward, rude and odd questions before anyone real sees it.

  4. 04

    Go live and watch.

    We read the conversations, fix gaps and report what it handled.

Questions about digital support

Will customers know it is AI?

Yes. It says so at the start. People are fine with it when it is quick and useful, and when they can reach a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

We test it hard first, limit it to what you have approved, and review conversations after launch. When it is unsure it hands over instead of guessing.

Does it need our customer data?

Only what the job needs. We agree what it can see before launch. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm data handling and where data is stored]

Can it work for staff only?

Yes. Many businesses start there because it carries less risk and shows quick value.

What does it cost?

It is scoped to your business, so there is no list price. Book a call and we will talk through what it would take.
