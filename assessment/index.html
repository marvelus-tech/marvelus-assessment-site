Why ask the team

The owner knows it is slow. The team knows why.

Owners usually feel where the time goes, but not which tasks, how often or how long. The people doing the work can tell you. So we ask them, one at a time, in a short, friendly chat.

The result is a list you can trust, because it comes from the people who do the tasks, not from a guess.

The maths

Every answer becomes a dollar figure.

We ask "about how many times a week, about how many minutes each". Counting events works better than asking people to estimate how long something takes.

times a week × minutes each × 52 × pay rate = dollars a year

We use your own pay rates. If you would rather not share them, we use the Victorian average.

Straight talk: hours only count as dollars when spend stops, a hire is avoided, or waiting work gets done. Recovered time is capacity, not cash, until it is put to use. The report says which is which.

Questions about the staff-level assessment

What is the Staff-level AI Assessment?

A paid assessment for businesses with roughly 15 to 150 staff. We ask the people doing the work, not only the owner, where their time goes. You get a findings report that puts every repeat task in hours and dollars a year, ranked, with no names, and we present it to your leadership.

Who is it for?

Owner-led businesses, or ones run by an owner and a general manager, with roughly 15 to 150 staff and a lot of repeatable admin such as quotes, bookings, scheduling, invoicing and enquiries. Under 15 staff, the Business Assessment is a better fit.

How is it priced?

How much of my team's time does it take?

A survey of about 5 minutes and a 15 minute chat for each person. One call for the owner. That is all we ask of your team.

It is a relaxed conversation about one person's week. We ask which tasks repeat, about how many times a week, and about how many minutes each takes. Counting events works better than asking people to guess how long something takes. We open every chat by saying it is about tasks, not jobs.
