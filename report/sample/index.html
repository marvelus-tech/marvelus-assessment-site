Five dimensions, each on a five-point ladder from ad hoc to optimised. The score is the average level shown as a share of the top level: 3.0 of 5, which is 60 out of 100.

4. The maths

Hours a week × hourly rate × 4.33 weeks in a month = cost a month. Multiply by 12 for the year. Every figure traces to something said on the call. Missing facts stay unknown. Any effect on sales is counted at gross profit, not revenue. If hours just disappear into the day, the number is zero.

5. What to do first

  1. Save your five best recent quotes in one folder, then draft new quotes from them.
  2. Write out-of-hours replies for the five most common enquiries and send them automatically.
  3. Move supplier orders to one template for your top three suppliers.

6. What to leave alone

Weekly reporting built by hand: it takes 2 hours, nobody reads it and removing it saves more than automating it. Delete it first.
