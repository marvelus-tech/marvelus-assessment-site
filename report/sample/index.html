2. How to read this report

  • It is about tasks, not jobs. Nobody is being assessed.
  • Answers are grouped by task and role. There are no names.
  • Each figure is times a week × minutes each × 52 × pay rate.
  • Where the report says "capacity until used", the time comes back to the day. It becomes dollars only when something changes because of it.

5. What to do first

  1. Draft quotes from the five most recent similar jobs, so the estimator starts from a draft, not a blank page.
  2. Stop retyping job details: pass them once from the job record to the accounting software.
  3. Write standard answers to the most common customer questions and use them every time.
