Pricing

Clear about how we price, and a clear place to start.

The staff-level assessment is priced by headcount. If you want a smaller first step, the Business Assessment is a fixed price. Anything after the findings is scoped separately and in writing, so you know the cost before you agree to it.

What drives the cost

  • How many people take part, for the staff-level assessment.

Pricing questions

How is the staff-level assessment priced?

Priced by headcount. We do not publish figures. Book the 10-minute chat and we will quote once we know how many people take part.

Why is the Business Assessment paid?

Because the report is custom, and a paid piece of work gets our full attention. It is a fixed price so there are no surprises.

Is GST included in the Business Assessment price?

No. The price is $1,000 AUD plus GST. We write it as $1,000 + GST.

What currency are prices in?

Australian dollars.
