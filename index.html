How it works

The maths

Every answer becomes a dollar figure.

We ask "about how many times a week, about how many minutes each". Counting events works better than asking people to estimate how long something takes.

times a week × minutes each × 52 × pay rate = dollars a year

We use your own pay rates. If you would rather not share them, we use the Victorian average.

Straight talk: hours only count as dollars when spend stops, a hire is avoided, or waiting work gets done. Recovered time is capacity, not cash, until it is put to use. The report says which is which.

The promise

Tasks, not jobs.

When staff worry about their jobs, the honest answers dry up. So every chat opens the same way: this is about tasks, not jobs, and nobody is being assessed.

  • Answers are grouped by task and role, never by name.
  • No names in the report.
  • The aim is to remove grunt work so people can do more interesting work.
  • Time cost to your team: a 5 minute survey and a 15 minute chat each, and one call for the owner.

Questions, answered.

What is the Staff-level AI Assessment?

A paid assessment for businesses with roughly 15 to 150 staff. We start with a 10-minute chat with the owner. Then each person on your team does a short survey of about 5 minutes and a 15 minute chat. You get a findings report that lists every task in hours and dollars a year, ranked, with no names, and a presentation to leadership.

Why ask the staff and not only the owner?

The people doing the work know where the time goes. The owner usually knows something is slow, but not which tasks, how often or how long. Asking the team gets the real picture.

What does it cost?

The staff-level assessment is priced by headcount. We do not publish figures. Book the 10-minute chat and we will quote once we know your headcount. The smaller first step, the Business Assessment, is $1,000 AUD + GST, once, and GST is not included in the $1,000. Digital support and SEO are scoped to your business.

How do the staff chats and consent work?

Each chat is about 15 minutes. It is recorded with an AI note taker only when the person says yes first. Answers are grouped by task and role, never by name. See the staff-level assessment page for the full detail.

What is the 15 minute intake call?

That is the Business Assessment, the smaller first step at $1,000 AUD + GST. You phone in and an AI voice agent runs a guided conversation of about 15 minutes about how the business runs. We email a written report with a score out of 100, ranked opportunities and a dollar cost for each, the same business day as your call. It says it is an AI voice agent at the start of the call. How do you work out the dollar figures?

Simple maths you can check. Times a week, times minutes each, times 52, times the pay rate. We use your pay rates, or the Victorian average if you would rather not share them. Hours only count as dollars when spend stops, a hire is avoided or waiting work gets done.

What if the answer is "do not use AI"?

Then we say so. Sometimes the fix is a changed process, a setting in software you already pay for, or a better form. A report that tells you where not to spend is still worth having.
