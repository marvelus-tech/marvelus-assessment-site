How it works

From a phone call to a ranked list, in four steps.

The Business Assessment is a paid, one-off piece of work. It starts with a short call and ends with a report you can act on, whether or not you ever hire us.

  1. 01

    Pay once.

    $1,000 AUD + GST through secure checkout. No subscription and no booking link to chase.

  2. 02

    Call when you are ready.

    After payment you see a call-in number and your own access code. Ring any time that suits you. An AI voice agent asks the questions and follows your answers. It takes about 15 minutes.

  3. 03

    We build your report.

    From what you said, we work out where your hours go, what that costs, and which fixes are worth doing first.

  4. 04

    You decide.

    You get a written report and the call transcript. Use it yourself, hand it to your team, or ask us to help. It is your choice.

Questions, answered.

What is the Business Assessment?

A paid, one-off review of how your business spends its time. You phone in, an AI voice agent runs a guided conversation of about 15 minutes, and we turn your answers into a written report. The report gives you a score out of 100, a ranked list of opportunities and a dollar cost for each.

Is the voice agent a real person?

No. It is an AI voice agent, and it says so at the start of the call. It is built to ask follow-up questions on what you tell it, the way a good interviewer would. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm who at MarvelUs reviews each report, and how, before saying a person stands behind it]

What does it cost?

The Business Assessment is $1,000 AUD + GST, once. GST is not included in the $1,000. The free preview quiz costs nothing. Digital support and SEO are scoped to your business, so there is no list price. Book a call and we will talk it through.

How do you work out the dollar figures?

Simple maths you can check. Hours a week, times the hourly value of that work, times 4.33 weeks in a month. If you tell us 18 hours a week at $50 an hour, that is $3,897 a month. The report shows every number and where it came from. Hours only count as money if spend stops, a planned hire is avoided or waiting demand gets served.

What if the answer is "do not use AI"?

Then we say so. Sometimes the fix is a changed process, a setting in software you already pay for, or a better form. A report that tells you where not to spend is still worth having.

Who is this for?

Owner-run and small to mid-sized businesses: trades, hospitality, professional services, health, retail and others. If a handful of people spend a lot of their week on repeated admin, enquiries or follow-up, it is a good fit.

How long until I see results?

The preview takes about a minute. The paid assessment takes about 15 minutes on the phone, and we email your report the same business day as your call.

What happens to my information?

It stays with you and us. We do not sell it, share it or reuse it. The transcript is yours. The free preview runs in your browser and sends nothing. See our Privacy page, which is still a draft.
